How to read the prices

PITI break-even
The price where rent exactly covers principal, interest, property tax and insurance. An upper bound: it ignores vacancy and upkeep.
All-in break-even
The price where cash flow is $0 after PITI plus vacancy ({{ a.assumptions.vacancy_pct.value | int }}%), management ({{ a.assumptions.management_pct.value | int }}%) and a maintenance reserve ({{ a.assumptions.capex_reserve_pct.value | int }}% of price per year).
Target price
The highest price that still leaves your cash-flow goal each month. Prices are rounded down to the nearest $1,000.
